# Executive Summary

This project analyzes historical retail sales data to identify opportunities for increased profitability and business growth.

## Current Recommendations

1. Continue investing in Technology products due to their strong profitability.
2. Investigate Furniture performance to identify improvement opportunities.
3. Review discount strategies to determine whether increased sales justify reduced profit margins.
4. Explore opportunities to grow the Home Office customer segment because of its high average profit per order.
5. Prioritize future investment in the West region while validating market potential with additional external data.
