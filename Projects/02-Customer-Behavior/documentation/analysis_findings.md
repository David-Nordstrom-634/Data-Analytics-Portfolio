# Analysis Findings

## Investigation 1 - Customer Value & Purchase Frequency

### Business Question

How valuable and active are identified customers, and how frequently do they return to make additional purchases?

### Key Metrics

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Identified Customers | 5,942 |
| Repeat Customers | 4,255 |
| Repeat Customer Rate | 71.61% |
| Average Gross Sales per Customer | $2,986.10 |
| Average Net Sales per Customer | $2,801.80 |
| Average Completed Purchases per Customer | 6.22 |
| Average Days Between Purchases | 102.09 |

### Repeat Behavior Findings
With a repeat-customer rate of 71.61%, I would cautiously suggest that the retailer demonstrates strong repeat-purchase behavior. There are many factors that could contribute to this, such as product satisfaction, necessity, pricing, service, promotions, etc.

### Customer Value Findings
The average distance of about $184 between gross and net sales suggests that cancellations/returns reduce customer value. Further analysis at customer-level specifics would be needed to confirm how the size of cancellations/returns effects customer value.

### Purchase Timing Findings
With a 102-day average time between customer purchases, we can see that most customers make repeat purchases every 3-4 months. We could look further into wheter repeat purchases are replacements or completely new products to see more of the "why" customers return.
