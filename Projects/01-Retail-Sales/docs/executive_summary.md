# Executive Summary

Between 2014 and 2017, the business demonstrated consistent growth in both sales and profitability. Total sales increased by $248,967.76 while total profit increased by $43,895.30 over the four-year period. Technology was the top-performing category in three of the four years, while the West region consistently led the company in both sales and profit.

## Key Findings
- 2017 recorded the highest total sales and total profit during the analysis period.
- Technology consistently outperfomed other product categories in both sales and profitability.
- The West region generated the strongest financial performance in three of the four years analyzed.
- Orders with no discount or a 20% discount generally produced the strongest profitability over time.

## Recommendations
- Continue investing in the Technology category by prioritizing inventory, marketing, and product development.
- Expand successful sales strategies used in the West region to identify whether similar approaches can improve performance in other regions.
- Review discount policies above 20% to determine whether increased sales volume offsets lower profit margins.

## Limitations
- Historical data only, no current sales data
- No marketing data
- No inventory costs
- No competitor information
- No regional demographics
